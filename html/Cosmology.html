Cosmology

Embark on a journey through the vast expanse of the cosmos, where mysteries abound and wonders await exploration. As Carl Sagan famously said, 'The cosmos is within us. We are made of star-stuff.' In the pursuit of understanding our place in the universe, we echo the sentiment of Vera Rubin, who remarked, 'I think of the universe as the cosmos, and all of it is mysterious.' Through the lens of cosmology, we seek not only answers but also the questions that propel us forward, echoing the words of Neil deGrasse Tyson: 'The universe is under no obligation to make sense to you.' Let us marvel at the beauty and complexity of the cosmos, for as Albert Einstein once mused, 'The most incomprehensible thing about the universe is that it is comprehensible'.
